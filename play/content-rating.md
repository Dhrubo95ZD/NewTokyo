# Content rating declaration guide

Declare honestly in Play Console:

- Simulated gambling: yes (blackjack, roulette and slots; virtual Ledger Credits only).
- Real gambling or cash prizes: no.
- Crime references: yes, frequent, fictional.
- Violence: player combat is described through text and interface feedback; declare the strongest depiction actually present in the submitted build.
- User-generated content: yes (world chat, forums, mail and families).
- Users can interact: yes.
- Reporting/blocking/muting: yes.
- Location sharing: no.
- Intended age: 18+.

Do not describe Ledger Credits as cryptocurrency, money, withdrawable value, or a purchase reward.
