Blackwood City remembers every decision.

Create your character and build a permanent record in a connected online crime role-playing game. Complete story missions, develop a career, train your abilities, collect equipment, join a player family and earn your place in the city.

BUILD YOUR NAME
Follow a guided introduction, improve combat and crime skills, interview for professional careers, purchase property and complete long-term campaign chapters. Your progress is stored online and carries across sessions.

RUN DISTRICT OPERATIONS
Take on multi-stage operations across eight Blackwood districts. Gather intelligence, prepare your route, confront computer-controlled crews and complete the extraction. Operations do not use energy. District heat and reward efficiency provide fair guardrails while permanent mastery ensures that every attempt advances your account.

COLLECT AND EQUIP
Discover more than 200 documented items with distinct artwork, equipment slots, visible rarity tiers and published acquisition chances. Build a loadout around attack, defence, speed and dexterity, then pursue rare relics through missions, combat and repeatable city activity.

JOIN A REAL ONLINE CITY
Form or join a family with authenticated players, participate in family-supported operations, trade through the secured player market and compare permanent records in city rankings. World chat, private messages and forums include reporting, muting and blocking controls. Moretti does not populate social features with fake player profiles.

BUILD A CAREER
Interview for professions, complete shifts, gain workplace attributes and earn promotions. Skilled virtual market traders can qualify for an invitation to Federal Trust's finance career.

STUDY VIRTUAL MARKETS
Open an in-game brokerage account, choose leverage and lot size, study provider-backed Forex and metals charts and manage virtual positions. Trading uses play-earned Ledger Credits. Ledger Credits have no cash value; market data may be delayed and is not financial advice or real trade execution.

VISIT THE ARCADE
Rossi's Arcade contains three classic rooms settled by the city server: blackjack, slots and roulette. Arcade activity uses play-earned Arcade Dollars only. Arcade Dollars cannot be bought with city cash or real money, transferred outside the game or withdrawn. Verified net Arcade wins may be redeemed one-way into ordinary in-game cash at 100 Arcade Dollars = $50 city cash. There is no cash-to-Arcade conversion and no real-money value.

SUPPORT BLACKWOOD (OPTIONAL IN-APP PURCHASES)
The Supporter Store offers direct character-card frames and paper treatments through Google Play, plus an optional Moretti Monthly subscription. The membership provides one cosmetic Style Ticket per day, early cosmetic rotations and one extra public showcase slot. These purchases do not grant city cash, Arcade Dollars, Ledger Credits, weapons, stats, XP, energy, loot or combat power. Prices are shown in your local Google Play currency. No loot boxes, real-money gambling, cash packs or withdrawals are offered.
