# App access instructions for Play review

Moretti: Blackwood City requires Google sign-in and has no guest mode. In Play Console → App content → App access:

1. Select that all or some functionality is restricted.
2. Provide a dedicated Google test account controlled by the developer.
3. Include its sign-in instructions and any password/2-step-verification handling required by Google Play reviewers.
4. The reviewer should create a codename, complete or skip the guided tutorial, and use avatar → Help & Safety to verify account deletion controls.

Never place test credentials in this repository. Store them only in Play Console’s encrypted reviewer instructions.
