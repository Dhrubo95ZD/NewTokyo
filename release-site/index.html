Player support

Official privacy, safety, support, and account-control information for the Android game Moretti: Blackwood City.

Arcade Dollars are play-only. They cannot be bought with city cash or real money; verified net Arcade wins convert one-way into ordinary in-game cash at 100 Arcade Dollars = $50 city cash. Rossi's Arcade contains Blackjack, Slots and Roulette; nothing can be withdrawn as real money.
